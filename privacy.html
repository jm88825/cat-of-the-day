Privacy Policy — Cat of the Day

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Cat of the Day ("the app") shows one popular cat picture or video per day, plus

an archive of previous days.

We collect no personal data

  • The app has **no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no

tracking SDKs**.

  • The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information, device

identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or usage data.

  • The app requests no special permissions beyond internet access.

What the app downloads

To work, the app downloads:

  • A small public data file (JSON) describing the day's cat, hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • The picture or video itself, directly from the site where it was originally posted (for example Reddit's media servers i.redd.it / v.redd.it, or Imgur).

Like any web request, these servers receive standard technical information

(such as your IP address) needed to deliver the file. The app does not send them

anything else. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies:

GitHub,

Reddit,

Imgur.

Links and sharing

Tapping "view original" opens the original post in your browser or the source

app. Tapping "Share" opens your device's standard share sheet; the app does not

see what you share or with whom.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Content

All pictures and videos belong to their original creators and are credited with

a link to the original post. To request removal of content, contact us at the

address below.
